Website Choice

Business Name: Musa Meat

Musa Meat is a local farming business that focuses on supplying fresh, high-quality beef products direct to customers. The business aims to provide affordable and reliable meat to households, restaurants, and small businesses within the community. Musa Meat ensures that all products are fresh and sourced locally, promoting healthy consumption and supporting local agriculture. In today’s digital age, many customers rely on the internet to search for products and services. Musa Meat currently operates without a strong online presence, which limits its ability to reach a wider audience. A website will allow the business showcase its products, provide pricing information, and improve communication with customers. The website will include key pages such as the homepage, about page, products page, enquiry page, and contact page. These pages will provide important information about the business and make it easier for customers to interact with Musa Meat. The target audience includes local households, restaurants, and small businesses that require fresh and affordable meat products. Reasons for Choosing This Business

Musa Meat was chosen because it represents a realistic small business that can benefit from an online presence. A website will improve communication, attract customers, and support business growth. Musa Meat represents a realistic small business that can benefit from having a Website. Many small businesses do not have an online platform, which limits their growth and visibility. One of the main reasons for selecting Musa Meat is that it allows the development of a practical and functional website that solves real-world problems. Customers often struggle to find information about products, prices, and contact details when a business does not have a website. Creating a website for Musa Meat will improve communication and make it easier for customers to access information. It will also help attract more customers and increase business growth. Additionally, this project allows the use of web development skills such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create a user-friendly and responsive website.
